Privacy, plainly.

Updated 15 November 2026.

This website

No analytics, advertising trackers, accounts, cookies or email collection are configured. Demo queries stay in your browser and are not sent to a conjugation service. No waitlist submissions are stored. The theme toggle lasts only for the current page session.

GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. External links, including GitHub issues, follow their providers’ privacy policies. Do not post private information in public issues.

The extension

VerbeFlow stores the selected verb, tense, theme and up to ten favourites locally in the browser. It has no account or analytics service. Optional Auto appearance can process a screenshot of the visible active page locally to classify light/dark appearance. Screenshots are not stored or uploaded. This website does not inspect your tabs or request screenshot access.

French pronunciation uses an available browser or operating-system voice, preferring a local French voice. Voice providers may have their own network and privacy behavior.

Future early access

Email signup is not open. If a hosted provider is configured later, the signup link will explicitly identify it as external and point visitors to its privacy notice. The website will not pretend to save submissions.

Contact and details

Contact the maintainer through GitHub issues or the creator’s website. Read the extension privacy policy.
