# VerbeFlow Pro: validation before monetization

## Audience and problem
French learners reading online, especially self-directed A1–B2 learners, need a
quick explanation of an unfamiliar verb without abandoning their reading.
Validate this audience in interviews; proficiency levels are a hypothesis.

## Free and proposed Pro
Keep current conjugation lookup, offline reference, examples and ten favourites
free. Explore Pro for personal collections, retrieval quizzes, spaced repetition
and progress history. None of these proposed practice features exists yet.

## Experimental pricing
€3.99/month and €19 lifetime are research anchors, not purchasable offers.
Lifetime is about five months of subscription revenue and could undermine
recurring income; test it separately, cap a founding offer if pursued, and avoid
promising indefinite service without a cost plan. At €3.99, €50–300 gross/month
would require roughly 13–76 active monthly subscribers, before fees, taxes,
refunds and churn. This is arithmetic, not a revenue forecast.

## Differentiation and acquisition
A quiet side panel, offline access, explicit conjugation ambiguity and transparent
reference limitations distinguish the current product. Test learner communities
where promotion is allowed, French teachers, useful search-indexed examples,
GitHub discovery and short real demonstrations. Do not buy ads before validating
retention. Manual extension installation is a significant acquisition barrier.

## Thirty-day validation
Days 1–7: interview 8–10 learners, observe installation and lookup, fix friction.
Days 8–14: recruit 15–20 voluntary testers; ask which practice problem they have.
Days 15–21: show a clickable quiz/collection prototype and both price hypotheses.
Days 22–30: seek explicit willingness to pay and a second week of voluntary use.
Do not charge or accept preorders in this phase.

## Metrics and decision gates
Record aggregate interview observations with consent, never private browsing
content. No website analytics are configured, so visits and conversion rates
cannot currently be claimed. If a hosted waitlist is activated, count opt-ins,
source channel and unsubscribes through the provider. A GitHub issue is feedback,
not proof of purchase intent. Proceed only if at least 10 target learners test,
5 return voluntarily in a second week, and 5 explicitly accept a specific price
for the same clearly defined practice feature. These small-sample gates justify
another experiment, not a revenue projection. Otherwise revise or stop.

## Costs, risks and next decisions
Static GitHub Pages hosting and packaged data need no paid infrastructure in this
phase. Existing free hosting remains subject to provider limits. No email
provider is selected; evaluate its free tier, consent, deletion and export before
activation. Future payments, tax compliance, support and synchronization have
unknown costs and require separate decisions. Time for human linguistic review
and support is a real cost even with no hosting bill.

Primary risks: incomplete independent language review; reference-only entries
lack meanings/examples/compound auxiliaries; manual installation; unclear paid
value; lifetime support liability; browser/voice differences; licensed data
redistribution; and optional screenshot permissions reducing trust. Prioritize
content quality and evidence of repeated practice demand before payments or
accounts. Do not reclassify existing free favourites as a new paid feature.
