# VerbeFlow privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026.

VerbeFlow is a French verb reference and learning extension maintained by
Md Zunayedul Islam. It has no account system, advertising or analytics.

## Saved choices

Your selected verb, tense, light/dark preference and up to ten saved verbs are
stored locally in your browser. VerbeFlow does not send this information to the
maintainer or an analytics service. Removing the extension removes its browser
storage. You can remove saved verbs individually with their star buttons.

## Automatic appearance detection

If you use Auto appearance, VerbeFlow can briefly capture the visible active
tab to determine whether it is light or dark. The screenshot may include
whatever is visible on that webpage, including personal content. Processing
takes place locally in your browser. Screenshots are not saved, logged,
uploaded or shared. Only the resulting light/dark classification is used.

Across-site detection requires optional website access, requested only when
you click Enable automatic page detection and approve the browser prompt.
Without that access, detection is limited by the browser's temporary active-tab
permission; when unavailable, Auto falls back to browser appearance. Manual
light/dark mode does not request screenshots. You may revoke optional access
through your browser's extension settings.

## Pronunciation and links

Listen sends the displayed French example to a French voice provided through
your browser or operating system. VerbeFlow prefers a local French voice where
available, but the voice provider may use an online service. That provider's
privacy terms apply. VerbeFlow does not independently record or transmit speech.

Dictionary links and the creator's website open only when you click them. Their
own privacy policies apply. Verb data, fonts and artwork are packaged with the
extension; VerbeFlow does not fetch conjugations or remote code at runtime.

## Sharing and contact

The maintainer does not receive, sell or share your stored choices or webpage
screenshots. Browser and operating-system providers may process information
under their own policies, outside VerbeFlow's control.

For privacy questions, use the contact information on
[the creator's website](https://zunaaaaayed.github.io), or open an issue in
[the project repository](https://github.com/zunaaaaayed/VerbeFlow/issues).
Do not include private webpage content or personal information in public issues.
