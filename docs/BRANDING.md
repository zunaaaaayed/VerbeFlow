# VerbeFlow brand assets

The book-shaped V with an orange flowing stroke was generated with the built-in image_gen tool, then exported to Chrome's 16, 32, 48 and 128 px PNG sizes with macOS sips. The full-resolution generated master is assets/brand/verbeflow-master.png. It is used by the side-panel header, toolbar action, extension manager and panel favicon. The personal Z creator mark remains separate.

The wordmark is live Cormorant Garamond text, bundled under SIL OFL 1.1 in assets/fonts. Keeping the wordmark out of the bitmap preserves readability and accessible text at narrow widths.

## Initial generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production app icon and UI brand mark for VerbeFlow, an elegant French conjugation learning Chrome extension.
Create one single square, exceptionally clean, minimal, recognizable logo: a strong ivory V formed by two subtly curved open-book pages, with the right page continuing into one restrained warm-orange flowing stroke. A sophisticated editorial monogram, balanced and calm. Bold, simple geometry and generous negative space; legible at 16 pixels. No thin ornament, no lettering other than the implicit V, no wordmark, no shadows, no gradients, no mockup, no extra symbols.
Palette: solid deep blue #244855 rounded-square tile, cream #FBE9D0 main mark, a small vermilion #E64833 accent. Use flat precise color fills and crisp edges with a vector-like finish. Center the mark and fill most of the tile. Tile covers 94% of the square image with softly rounded corners; transparent outside the tile. The central V/book symbol should remain clear at tiny extension-toolbar sizes. Deliver only this single finished logo, not a logo sheet.

## Final refinement prompt

Refine the generated VerbeFlow icon. Preserve exactly the cream V shaped like two open-book pages and the restrained orange flowing stroke above the right page. Replace ALL background, including the interior gaps around the V, with one perfectly uniform opaque solid deep-blue #244855 square. No transparency anywhere, no gradients, no shadows, no glow, no texture. Flat cream #FBE9D0 and orange #E64833 fills, crisp refined logo edges. Keep the balanced centered design, generous padding, and bold small-size readability. One square app icon, no words or extra symbols.
